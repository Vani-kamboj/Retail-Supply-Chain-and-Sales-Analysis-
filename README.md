# Retail Supply Chain and Sales Analysis

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing North America's retail supply 
chain and sales performance. Designed to deliver actionable business insights 
through dynamic filters and visual storytelling.

## Tools Used
- Power BI

## Dashboard Highlights
![Architecture](PowerBI_dashboard.pdf)

### KPI Overview
Tracks current year vs previous year performance for:
- Sales, Orders, Profit, and Customers with YoY growth percentages

### Sales Trends
Year over year sales comparison from 2023 to 2026(jan).

### Leading Categories: YoY Sales Performance
Compares previous year vs current year sales across categories like 
Toys, Video Games, Pet Supplies, Sporting Goods, Women's Clothing and more 
with growth percentages.

### Business Overview by Regions
Regional breakdown of Total Sales, Profit, Orders and Customers 
across East, West, North, South, Central and Other regions.

### Customer Segmentation
Pie chart showing sales contribution by segment:
- Consumer, Corporate and Home Office

### Ranking Overview
Dynamic Top/Bottom ranking by Category, City, Customer and Department 
across Orders, Profit and Sales metrics.
